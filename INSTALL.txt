REVA Studio 0.1.0 — Preview

macOS 14 or later. Universal: Intel and Apple Silicon.

1. Install FFmpeg and ffprobe separately (for example: brew install ffmpeg).
2. Drag REVA Studio.app into Applications.
3. Open the app and grant permissions only for the inputs you choose to use.

This preview is ad hoc signed and not Apple-notarized.
If macOS blocks it, review the source/checksums and follow Apple's supported
Privacy & Security > Open Anyway flow. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Source, help and known limitations:
https://github.com/morf3uzzz/reva-studio
